## What Is TraderFriend?

TraderFriend is a crypto-powered trading and prediction platform that combines sports-outcome markets, high-leverage stock-style trading, and a long-term decentralized prediction network powered by Bittensor.

The platform is designed to make complex markets easier to understand through clear probabilities, real-time price movement, transparent trade information, and a straightforward user experience.

TraderFriend currently focuses on two primary products:

1. Sports Trading — prediction-based trading on football match outcomes.
2. 1001× Trading — short-duration, high-leverage directional trading on market-style price movements.

The long-term goal is to connect these products to decentralized intelligence produced and evaluated through the Bittensor ecosystem.

## Core Products

### Sports Trading

TraderFriend’s sports platform currently focuses on football.

Users can explore upcoming and live matches, compare predicted probabilities, monitor market movement, and trade match outcomes.

Core sports-trading features include:

• Upcoming and live football matches  
• Coverage of multiple leagues and competitions  
• Predicted win, draw, and loss probabilities  
• Live probability and market movement  
• Moneyline-style outcome markets  
• Simple Yes/No prices  
• TAO-denominated balances and trades  
• Open and completed position tracking  
• Watchlists and match monitoring  
• Trading history  
• Crypto deposits and withdrawals  
• User support  

Prices are presented in a simple format intended to help users understand the market-implied probability of each outcome.

### 1001× Trading

TraderFriend’s 1001× product is a high-speed, stock-style directional trading experience.

Users predict whether a displayed market price will move up or down. They can select their position size and leverage before entering a trade.

The interface supports leverage ranging from 1× to 1001×.

Features presented through the 1001× interface include:

• Live tick-based price charts  
• Multiple chart intervals  
• Buy and Sell positions  
• Adjustable leverage  
• Market orders  
• Limit orders  
• Take Profit and Stop Loss controls  
• Knockout and liquidation calculations  
• Maximum payout calculations  
• Open-position monitoring  
• Recent-trade information  
• Trade history  
• Round and sequence information  
• Public proof records  
• Trading rules  
• TAO-based position sizing  

The product is designed as a high-risk directional trading or betting experience. Higher leverage creates a smaller knockout range and significantly increases the possibility of losing the entire position.

1001× is a market-style product and does not represent ownership of shares in an underlying company unless a specific market explicitly states otherwise.

Demo and live-market availability may vary depending on the product’s development stage, user account, and jurisdiction.

## Decentralized Prediction Vision

TraderFriend’s long-term vision is to create a global, decentralized prediction ecosystem powered by Bittensor.

Instead of relying on predictions generated by one company or a single proprietary model, independent miners will generate forecasts using different:

• Sports-data sources  
• Financial and market data  
• Statistical methods  
• Artificial-intelligence models  
• Historical datasets  
• Real-time signals  
• Prediction strategies  

Validators will evaluate the quality, reliability, and historical accuracy of those forecasts.

TraderFriend will aggregate the strongest results and transform them into clear information that users can apply to sports and market decisions.

A future prediction may include:

• Predicted outcome or price direction  
• Probability or confidence score  
• Supporting market information  
• Model-performance history  
• Agreement between independent models  
• Relevant risk indicators  

This decentralized approach is intended to encourage competition between prediction models, improve transparency, and reduce dependence on a single centralized source of information.

## Bittensor and TAO

Bittensor is an open network where independent subnets produce digital commodities such as compute, inference, storage, and prediction.

Within the Bittensor ecosystem:

• Miners produce predictions, intelligence, or other useful outputs.  
• Validators evaluate and score miner performance.  
• Subnet operators design the rules and incentive mechanisms.  
• TAO supports incentives and participation across the network.  

TraderFriend intends to explore and develop Bittensor infrastructure for decentralized sports and market forecasting.

Learn more through the [official Bittensor documentation](https://www.bittensor.com/docs).
